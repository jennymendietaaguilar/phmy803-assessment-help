Answer key — Step 2

Correct answer: Favours VPA. Two of the four studies (Tang et al. 2017 and Marson et al. 2021) report VPA outperforming the comparator drugs, which is more than any other category. No category is empty, but "Favours VPA" has the strongest weight of evidence, suggesting the overall pattern leans towards VPA producing better seizure freedom/remission outcomes — with some inconsistency noted by Campos et al. and Nevitt et al.

Marson et al. (2021) reported higher remission rates for VPA than LEV. HoweverSignals a shift toward contradictory or inconsistent evidence — alerts the reader that not all studies agree., the findings were not entirely consistent. Campos et al. OverallIntroduces the writer's concluding judgement — pulls all the individual findings together into one overall pattern or claim., the evidence suggests that VPA generally produced more favourable seizure outcomes, although some variation in findings was observed across the four studies.
Why is this a synthesis? It identifies a pattern, integrates evidence from multiple studies, acknowledges contrasting findings, and draws an overall conclusion.
