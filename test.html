Introduction

When synthesising the literature, writers do not simply list individual study findings — they compare findings across studies to identify:

◎Areas of difference

Step 4: Compare with the model answer

Across the studies, evidence suggests that valproic acid (VPA) generally achieved better seizure freedom or remission outcomes than lamotrigine (LTG) and levetiracetam (LEV). Tang et al. (2017) found that VPA was associated with higher rates of seizure freedom than LTG, while Marson et al. (2021) reported higher remission rates for VPA than LEV. However, the findings were not entirely consistent. Campos et al. (2018) reported that outcomes varied according to seizure type, whereas Nevitt et al. (2022) found no significant differences in remission outcomes between medications. Overall, the evidence suggests that VPA generally produced more favourable seizure outcomes, although some variation in findings was observed across the four studies.
Why is this a synthesis? It identifies a pattern, integrates evidence from multiple studies, acknowledges contrasting findings, and draws an overall conclusion.
